8 Conclusion
Based on our findings, we can conclude that the NEAT-based control system is not a
viableandeffectivealternativetotraditionalPIDcontrollersinscenarioswherespeed
is the primary objective. While the NEAT controller demonstrated its adaptability
and achieved a significantly lower Root Mean Squared Error in line deviation com-

trade-off: PID is the better choice for high-speed systems where lap time is crucial,
whereas NEAT excels in applications where trajectory precision and reproducibility
are necessary, such as in industrial warehouses.
